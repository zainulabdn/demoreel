# Changelog

## 0.2.0

Sound.

- **Voice-over.** `"sound": { "voice": true }` speaks every caption. The recording waits for each line, so the voice sets the pace and two lines never overlap. A caption can say more than it shows (`"speak"`), stay silent (`"speak": false`), or play a clip you recorded yourself (`"audio"`).
- **Three voices.** A natural one (Kokoro, a free model that runs on your machine, set up once with `demoreel voice install`), the computer's built-in one (macOS, Windows, or espeak on Linux), or any text-to-speech command of your own. `demoreel voice` lists them and `demoreel voice try` lets you hear one.
- **Click and typing sounds**, generated by Demoreel and placed on the exact frame of each click and key press. Clicks are heard on the side of the page they happen on.
- **Music.** `"sound": { "music": "./track.mp3" }` adds a track that fades in and out and steps back while the voice speaks.
- The soundtrack is mixed to -16 LUFS, the level phones and social sites expect, and written into the MP4 as AAC by the browser's own encoder. Still nothing to install: ffmpeg is used only where the browser cannot encode AAC.
- **Subtitles.** Add `"srt"` to `output.formats` for a `.srt` file of the spoken lines (or the captions), ready to upload with the video.
- `doctor` reports whether this machine can make sound and which voices it has.
- `render` can change the music and the click sounds of a kept recording without recording again.
- New settings `DEMOREEL_VIDEO_ENCODER=ffmpeg` and `DEMOREEL_AUDIO_ENCODER=ffmpeg` hand encoding to ffmpeg on a machine where the browser's encoder misbehaves.
- A release workflow: pushing a version tag publishes a GitHub release from this changelog.
- Fixed: a change on the page that takes a single frame, such as text replaced with no animation, was missing from the video half the time, until something else on the page moved. Anything that repaints 30 times a second, such as a playing video, was recorded at 15. The recorder now looks at every frame.

Sound has been tested on macOS. On Windows and Linux the built-in voice and the natural voice installer are written but not yet tried on a real machine; please open an issue if one fails.

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Record a real web app from a JSON storyboard of plain-language steps: `click`, `type`, `press`, `hover`, `scroll`, `select`, `drag`, `goto`, `wait`, `waitFor`, `say`, `zoom` and `eval`.
- Elements are found by the names people see (button text, labels, placeholders, `aria-label`), with `css=`, `testid=`, `text=`, `label=` and `placeholder=` for the rest. Failures suggest close matches and list what is on screen.
- Automatic camera: zooms in as the cursor arrives, holds while typing, follows the part of the page that changed, and pulls back for scrolls, drags and page changes.
- Natural cursor paths, click ripples, arrow, hand and text cursors, and a touch dot for phone and tablet viewports.
- Captions, browser, window, phone and bare frames, eight backgrounds, custom colours and gradients.
- Output as MP4 (H.264), GIF, a poster image and a contact sheet, in 1080p, 720p, 1440p, square, portrait and vertical sizes. The MP4 is kept under 10 MB by default.
- `check` to test a storyboard in seconds, `render` to restyle a kept recording, `doctor` to check the machine, `init` for a starter file.
- `serve` for static folders, `start` to launch a dev server, `theme` for dark mode, `${env:NAME}` for secrets.
- Zero dependencies: drives an installed Chrome, Edge or Brave over the DevTools Protocol and encodes in the browser. Falls back to `ffmpeg` for MP4 when the browser has no H.264 encoder.
- Packaged as an Agent Skill, a Claude Code plugin and a Codex plugin.
